Abbay is a free audiobook player for the world's public-domain classics.

Search the LibriVox catalogue, thousands of books read by volunteers, and start listening in seconds. Download books to listen offline, jump between chapters, and pick up exactly where you left off.

• Free public-domain audiobooks from LibriVox: Austen, Doyle, Dickens, Shelley, Twain and many more
• Download for offline listening
• Chapter list and quick chapter switching
• Playback speed control
• Continue listening right from your library
• Lock screen and Control Center controls
• Import your own audiobook files from the Files app
• English and Portuguese

No ads. No account. No tracking. Abbay collects no data about you; everything stays on your iPhone.

Some people say that if a product is free, you're the product. Here, the developer is: Abbay is free and always will be. If you enjoy it, say hi. The link is in the app's settings.
